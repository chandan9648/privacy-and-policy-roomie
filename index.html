Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains how RoomieHisab handles information when you use the app to manage shared household expenses.

1. Information we use

When you sign in with Google, we receive account information made available by Google, such as your name, email address, profile photo and account identifier. We use this information to sign you in and identify your membership.

If you create or join a household, RoomieHisab stores information needed to run that household, which may include:

  • Household name, creator, creation time and invite code.
  • Member name, email, profile photo, role and join information.
  • Expenses you or household members enter, including description, amount, date, payer and optional notes.
  • Expense activity records, such as who added, edited or deleted an expense and when.
  • Settlement summaries, including the date range, totals, participants and suggested transfers.

The app also keeps account and household references needed to reopen your selected household. We do not ask you to provide precise location, contacts or payment-card details.

2. How we use information

We use this information to authenticate you, provide household access, display and calculate shared expenses and balances, create settlement summaries, maintain an activity history, and protect the service against unauthorized access.

3. Household sharing

Household information is shared with people who belong to that household. Members can see household expenses, member details, activity records and settlement summaries. Anyone with a valid invite code can use it to request membership. Keep invite codes private and only share them with people you intend to invite.

Household administrators can manage household settings and membership. Expense changes and deletions are restricted by app permissions to the payer or an administrator.

4. Service providers and disclosure

RoomieHisab uses Google sign-in and Firebase services (provided by Google) for authentication and cloud data storage. These providers process information to operate the features described in this policy, subject to their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or when necessary to protect users, the service or other people.

5. Reports and files

When you choose to export a PDF or Excel report, the app creates the file on your device and opens the Android share/save flow. You decide whether to save or share it, and which app or recipient receives it. A recipient's handling of a shared file is governed by their own practices.

6. Retention and deletion

Cloud information is kept while it is needed to provide the service and maintain household records. You can leave a household; an administrator may remove members. Leaving or being removed revokes household access but does not automatically erase expenses or activity history already recorded for that household. The household creator can delete the household, and administrators can manage its members. Account sign-out ends the app session but does not itself delete cloud records.

To request deletion of your account information or ask about household data, contact us using the address below. Some information may remain in household records or backups where needed for legitimate operational, security or legal reasons.

7. Security

We use Firebase security rules to require authentication and check household membership for household data access. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please protect your Google account and invite codes.

8. Children

RoomieHisab is intended for people who can legally use the service in their location. It is not designed to collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The latest version and its effective date will be available on this page.
